Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

Go Steady ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit gosteady.co (the "Site").

Information We Collect

When you sign up for updates on our Site, we collect:

  • Email address
  • First name (if provided)

We also automatically collect certain technical data when you visit the Site:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the Site
  • Referring website or source
  • Browser type and device information
  • Approximate geographic location (country/region level)

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you updates about Go Steady's launch and availability
  • To honor any promotional offers (e.g., early signup discounts)
  • To understand how visitors use our Site so we can improve it
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following third-party services on our Site:

  • Plausible Analytics — a privacy-friendly analytics tool that does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. It provides aggregate visitor statistics only.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook) — we use the Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our Facebook advertising. The Meta Pixel may use cookies to track conversions, optimize ads, and build targeted audiences. You can learn more about Meta's data practices at Meta's Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of interest-based advertising through your browser settings or by visiting Your Ad Choices.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Netlify — our hosting provider, which processes form submissions on our behalf
  • Meta (Facebook) — conversion event data to measure ad performance

Data Retention

We retain your email address and name for as long as needed to send you product updates. You can request removal at any time by emailing us.

Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us at contact@gosteady.co.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@gosteady.co.
