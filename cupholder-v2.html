Your questions, answered

Know exactly what GoSteady is—and is not.

Does GoSteady detect falls?

No. GoSteady records movement of the rollator. It does not detect falls, diagnose health conditions, or determine why activity changed.

What will happen when the alert control is pressed?

The planned Family Connect behavior is to notify the family members selected during setup so someone can call and check in. It will not contact 911 or a professional monitoring center, and there is no speaker or voice call on the device. This feature remains in development and must complete hardware and delivery validation before release.

Will it share location?

GoSteady does not currently collect GPS location. We are exploring consent-based location sharing only during a user-initiated family alert—not continuous tracking or route history. Privacy language and technical validation will be completed before that feature is released.

Does the older adult need a smartphone or Wi-Fi?

No. GoSteady has its own cellular connection. Family members use a phone or computer to view updates and manage notifications.

Does it work outside the home?

GoSteady travels with the rollator and uses cellular connectivity rather than home Wi-Fi. Connectivity depends on supported cellular coverage.

Does it need daily charging?

No. GoSteady is being designed for long battery life without a daily charging routine. We will publish a specific battery interval and service process only after final production validation.

Who receives the updates?

Only family members or trusted contacts invited to the Care Circle receive GoSteady updates. The person using the rollator should know about and consent to the service.

Will it fit the rollator?

The current clamp is designed for common four-wheel rollator frame tubing, but final compatibility specifications are still being validated. Before fulfillment, we will offer a photo-confirmation option for any uncertain frame.
