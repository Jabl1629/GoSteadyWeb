How it works

Three steps, then you stop thinking about it

1

Clamp it on

It attaches to the rollator frame with a thumb screw. No tools, about a minute. You can do it on your next visit.

2

They use the rollator normally

Coffee to the mailbox, water on a walk. Nothing about their routine changes, and there's nothing for them to charge or turn on.

3

You check in from your phone

Open the page and see how much they've moved today and this week — and whether that's normal for them.

What you'll see

Activity, trends, and a heads-up when things change

GoSteady measures how much the rollator is moving — how often, how far, and at what time of day. Over a couple of weeks it learns what a normal day looks like for your parent, then tells you when a day stops looking normal.

  • How much they moved today, and this week
  • Whether today is unusually quiet compared to their own normal
  • A text or email when movement drops off for long enough to matter
  • Optional location sharing, if you both want it on

Yes. $20/month covers the cellular connection and the family portal. It starts when your device ships in December 2026, not when you order, and you can cancel any time. If you cancel, the device stops reporting activity — though it still works as a cupholder.

When will it ship?

December 2026. If that date slips, we'll email you and you can take a full refund, no questions asked.
