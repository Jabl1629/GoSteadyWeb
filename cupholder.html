Before you order

Will it fit my parent's rollator?

Almost certainly. It clamps to standard rollator frame tubing, and every rollator style we've tried it on has fit. If you want to be sure, email a photo of the rollator to support@gosteady.co and we'll confirm before you order.

Is there a monthly fee?

Yes. $20/month covers the cellular connection and the family portal. It starts when your device ships in December 2026, not when you order, and you can cancel any time. If you cancel, the device stops reporting activity — though it still works as a cupholder.

When will it ship?

December 2026. If that date slips, we'll email you and you can take a full refund, no questions asked.

What if we don't like it?

Return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Before it ships, you can cancel any time for a full refund.

Does my parent need a smartphone or Wi-Fi?

No. The device has its own cellular connection. There is nothing to pair, no account for them to make, and no app on their phone. You see the data on your phone or computer.

Does it detect falls or call for help?

No. GoSteady is not a fall detector or an emergency-response system and will not call anyone for you. It shows activity patterns so you can notice changes early. For emergencies, pair it with a medical alert service.

Will my parent know it's there?

Yes, and we think that matters. It's a visible cupholder with a small light on it, not a hidden sensor. We'd encourage you to tell them what it does.
