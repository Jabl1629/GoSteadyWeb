Discreet design that doesn't draw attention

Go Steady replaces the standard walker foot, blending seamlessly into your loved one's daily routine. They'll never feel "monitored"—and you'll always have peace of mind.

  • Activity tracking shows daily mobility patterns
  • Instant sharing of activity alerts with caregivers
  • Green indicator ring confirms active connection
  • Weather-resistant for indoor and outdoor use

2 Year Battery

Set it and forget it. Our advanced low-power design means no weekly charging hassles.

Instant Install

Snaps onto any standard walker in seconds. No tools, no modifications, no waiting.

No Bluetooth Required

Works anywhere with cell coverage. No Wi-Fi setup, no Bluetooth pairing, no tech headaches.

Replaceable Caps

Swap between smooth-glide or high-traction caps to match any floor surface or preference.

Configure notifications for your peace of mind

Choose how and when you want to be notified. Go Steady sends smart alerts directly to your phone — from low activity warnings to weekly summaries — so you're always in the loop without being overwhelmed.

  • Share notifications with multiple family members
