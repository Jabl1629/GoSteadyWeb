What messages we send

Our messages are transactional and account-related — we do not send marketing or promotional texts. You may receive:

  • One-time login codes (2FA / verification) — a code to verify your mobile number and sign in securely.

How you opt in

You provide consent to receive these messages when you enter your mobile number and create or join an account in the GoSteady app — for example, when a walker user sets up a device, or when a family member or caregiver accepts an invitation to join a Care Circle. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. You confirm you are the account holder or authorized user of the mobile number you provide.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on how you use the Service — for instance, a login code each time you sign in, plus any account or activity notifications you have enabled.

GoSteady does not charge for text messages, but message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any GoSteady text to opt out; we will send one confirmation and then stop sending messages, except that you may still receive one-time login codes needed to secure your account. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at contact@gosteady.co. You can also manage notification preferences in the app.
